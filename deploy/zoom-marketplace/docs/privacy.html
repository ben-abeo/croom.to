Privacy policy

What the integration that lets Crystal Meet conference rooms join Zoom meetings accesses, what it keeps, and what it does not.

Who we are

Crystal Meet Rooms (the "integration") is software from Crystal PM ([TO CONFIRM: legal entity name], [TO CONFIRM: postal address]) that lets a conference room's Crystal Meet device join Zoom meetings. Crystal PM is responsible for the information the integration handles and is the controller of it. Questions about this policy go to support@crystalpm.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the integration only: the part of the Crystal Meet software on a room's device that joins Zoom meetings, and the two Zoom apps it uses (the Marketplace app "Crystal Meet Rooms" with Zoom's Meeting SDK, and a Server-to-Server OAuth app in the same Zoom account). It does not cover Zoom's own services, which Zoom's Privacy Statement covers, the room's calendar connection, or Crystal PM's other products and websites.

What the integration accesses

  • The room's own Zoom user. Through the account's Server-to-Server OAuth app, the device gets an access token for the Zoom account and, with it, a Zoom Access Key (ZAK) for the room's own Zoom user, for example room1@crystalpm.com, using the read-only scopes user:read:zak:admin and user:read:token:admin. The ZAK lets the room join a meeting as that user. The integration asks Zoom for nothing else: no other users, profiles, contacts, meeting lists, recordings, chats or reports.
  • The meeting the room joins. Through Zoom's Meeting SDK, the device joins a meeting as an ordinary participant, and only after a person in the room presses Join. It uses the meeting number and passcode from the meeting's link to join, appears under the room's name, receives the meeting's sound and video to play on the room's speaker and TV, and sends the room's camera and microphone unless someone in the room turns them off. Like any participant, it can see what Zoom shows participants, such as other participants' names.

What is kept, and where

  • On the room's device: one credentials file, /etc/croom/zoom-credentials.json, with the two apps' client IDs and secrets, the Zoom account ID and the room's Zoom user. Its file permissions let only the account that the Crystal Meet service runs as read it.
  • Short-lived tokens: the account access token stays in the Crystal Meet service's memory until it expires, within an hour. The room's ZAK is fetched for one join and is valid for at most two hours. Neither token is written to disk or to a log. The room's own browser receives the ZAK, the meeting's signature and its passcode from a one-time join page on the device, served with an instruction not to store it (no-store), so the browser keeps no copy of them in its profile.
  • In the device's service log: the meeting number when the room joins and leaves a meeting and, when a join fails, the link that was tried and Zoom's reason. The log stays on the device for troubleshooting and ages out with the operating system's log rotation.
  • Nowhere: the integration does not record, store or transcribe meeting audio, video, chat or shared screens, and keeps no participant names or lists. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends no Zoom information to Crystal PM's servers or anyone else.

How the information is used

Only to join the meeting that a person in the room asked to join, to show it on the room's TV, to make the room page's Mute, camera and Leave buttons work, and to troubleshoot the device.

How long it is kept

The credentials file stays on the device until it is deleted, as described under Removing the app. Tokens last no longer than their expiry: at most one hour for the account token and two hours for a ZAK. Log lines last until the device's log rotation removes them. Nothing else is kept.

Sharing

Crystal PM does not sell, rent or share information from the integration. The device sends information only to Zoom, as joining a meeting requires: the apps' credentials to obtain tokens, and the meeting number, passcode, signature, ZAK and the room's name to join. Zoom processes the meeting under its own Privacy Statement and the meeting host's settings, including any recording the host starts.

Security

  • Every connection from the device to Zoom uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • The credentials file can be read only by the account the Crystal Meet service runs as (file permissions 600).
  • The page that hosts the Meeting SDK runs on the device itself and answers only the device. Each join's details can be fetched from it only once, and are served with no-store, so the browser does not keep them.
  • The room page and the door sign are served only on the office network.

Your choices

People in the room can turn the room's camera and microphone off from the room page at any time, and leave the meeting. The account's administrator can remove the integration at any time, as described under Removing the app. Requests about information the integration handles go to support@crystalpm.com.

Children

The integration is used in business conference rooms. It is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the integration starts handling information differently, Crystal PM will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect, and will tell the organizations whose rooms use the integration about material changes.
