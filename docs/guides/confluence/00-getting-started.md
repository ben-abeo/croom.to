# Conference rooms: getting started

Our conference rooms run on **Crystal Meet**. A touch screen on the table runs the call, the TV shows it, and a sign by the door shows who has the room. **You don't need any technical experience, a login or a password.** If you can create a Google Calendar event, you can book a room and run a meeting in it.

This page is where to begin. Read it once (about five minutes), then use the pages below whenever you need a specific task.

## 1. Meet the room

| Equipment | Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Table screen** | The touch screen on the table | Join calls, mute, change the volume, move the camera, and book the room. |
| **TV** | On the wall | Shows the call. It turns on and switches input by itself; you never need to touch it. |
| **Camera and speaker** | The Logitech MeetUp under the TV | The room's camera, microphones and speaker in one. |
| **Door sign** | Outside the door | Shows whether the room is free and lets you book it on the spot. |

**What the colours mean.** The door sign's light bar and screen use the same four colours:

- **Green: free.** Walk in, or book it.
- **Yellow: starting soon.** A booking begins within 10 minutes.
- **Red: in use.** A meeting or booking is on now.
- **Grey or off: not connected.** The sign can't reach the room. Tell the administrator.

## 2. Three things to know first

> **The room never joins a call by itself.** Someone in the room always taps **Join now** on the table screen. The button opens 10 minutes before the start.
>
> **The room joins Google Meet and Zoom.** For Microsoft Teams, Webex or anything else, use your laptop. See [Teams, Webex and laptop meetings].
>
> **Meeting titles are public.** The title shows on the door sign and the TV. For a confidential meeting, use a plain title such as "Team meeting".

## 3. Your first meeting, start to finish

1. **Reserve the room.** In Google Calendar, create your event and add the room under the **Rooms** tab. See [Reserve a room in Google Calendar].
2. **Add the call.** Google Meet is added for you. For Zoom, paste the Zoom link into the event's **Location** or **Description**. See [Book a room with Google Meet] or [Book a room with Zoom].
3. **Join.** Up to 10 minutes before the start, tap **Join now** on the table screen. The TV shows the call in about 15 seconds. See [Join and run a call from the table].
4. **Leave.** Tap **Leave**, then **Tap again to leave**. Always leave from the table so the room is ready for the next person.

**Need the room right now?** Tap **Use now** on the door sign or the table screen. See [Book at the door sign or table screen].
