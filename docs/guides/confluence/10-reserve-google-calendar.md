# Reserve a room in Google Calendar

*Part of [Conference rooms: getting started].*

This is the best way to book ahead. The booking shows on the door sign and the table screen within about a minute.

- **Create your event** in Google Calendar as usual: title, date, time and guests.
- **Add the room.** Open the **Rooms** tab next to Guests (on the full event page, select **More options** first). Pick the conference room by name, for example `Room 1`.
- **Check that the room is available.** Google shows whether the room is free at that time. If it's taken, pick another time or room.
- **Select Save.** Google invites the room like any other guest.

*Within a minute or two you should see your meeting in the **Today** list on the table screen and on the door sign. Meetings more than 7 days away appear once they're within a week.*

> **GOOD TO KNOW**
>
> **If the room declines your invite,** someone else already has that time. The room ignores declined bookings, so pick another slot.
>
> **To change or cancel,** edit the event in Google Calendar. Bookings made in Google Calendar can only be changed there, not on the room's screens.
>
> **Can't find the room under Rooms?** A newly added room can take up to a day to show up in Google.
