# Book a room with Google Meet

*Part of [Conference rooms: getting started].*

- **Reserve the room** as in [Reserve a room in Google Calendar].
- **Leave the Google Meet video call on.** Google Calendar adds **Join with Google Meet** to new events automatically. If it's missing, select **Add Google Meet video conferencing**.
- **Save.** That's all. The room knows it's a Google Meet call.

*The table screen shows "Google Meet" under your meeting's title. To join, See [Join and run a call from the table].*

> **TIP**
>
> **Meeting in person, no call?** Remove the Google Meet link from the event. Otherwise the room shows a Join now button for a call nobody needs.
>
> **Outside guests in a Google Meet call?** If someone outside Crystal PM hosts the call, the room asks to join under its own name (for example "Room 1"). The host needs to let it in within 5 minutes.
