# Book at the door sign or table screen

*Part of [Conference rooms: getting started].*

Need the room right now, or want to book it while you're walking past? The door sign and the table screen both have **Use now** and **Book**. No login or PIN is needed. Bookings made here go into the room's Google calendar, but **not** into your own calendar.

## Use the room now

Shown when the room is free.

1. Tap **Use now** on the door sign or the table screen.
2. Type **Your name or the meeting's name**.
3. Pick how long. Each choice shows when it ends, for example `30 min · until 12:30 PM`. You can't pick a time that runs into the next booking.
4. Tap **Start**.

*You should see "The room is yours until 12:30 PM." The sign turns red.*

## Book a later time

1. Tap **Book** on the door sign, or **Book room** on the table screen.
2. Pick the day: **Today**, **Tomorrow** or one of the next five days.
3. Pick a start time. Only open times are shown, every 15 minutes from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
4. Type your name or the meeting's name, and choose **How long** (15 minutes to 4 hours).
5. Tap **Book**. To go back a step, tap **Change time** or **Back**.

*You should see "Booked: your name, 9:00 AM to 9:30 AM." It appears in the Today list (or in **This week** on the door sign).*

**FINISHED EARLY?**

## End now (table screen only)

For any meeting in progress, whether it was booked on the door sign, on the table or in Google Calendar: tap **End now**, then tap again within 5 seconds. This frees the room for others, on the door sign and in Google Calendar.

**PLANS CHANGED?**

## Cancel

A booking made on the room's screens that hasn't started has **Cancel** beside it. Tap it, then tap **Tap again to cancel** within 4 seconds.

> **GOOD TO KNOW**
>
> **Want a call with your booking?** Bookings made at the door or table have no Meet or Zoom link. To run a call, paste the link into **Join with a link** on the table (see [Join and run a call from the table]), or book in Google Calendar instead.
>
> **Everyone can cancel.** Anyone at the room can cancel a booking made on its screens. If yours matters, book it in Google Calendar.
>
> **Left alone,** the booking steps close by themselves after a minute.
