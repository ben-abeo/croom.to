# Join and run a call from the table

*Part of [Conference rooms: getting started].*

## Join a booked meeting

1. Look at the table screen. Until 10 minutes before the start, the button reads **Join opens at 9:50 AM**.
2. From 10 minutes before the start, tap **Join now**. You can also tap **Join** beside the meeting in the Today list.
3. The table shows "Joining…". The TV turns on and shows the call in about 15 seconds.

*The table switches to the meeting screen and shows the call's timer. If it says "Waiting for the host to let the room in.", ask the host to admit the room. They have 5 minutes.*

## Join a call that isn't booked

1. On the table's main screen, tap the box **Zoom meeting ID, Meet code or link**.
2. Type a Zoom meeting ID like `559 000 0559` or a Google Meet code like `abc-defg-hij` (the dashes are optional), or a whole link. Tap **123** on the keyboard for numbers.
3. If a **Passcode, if the meeting has one** box appears, type the passcode.
4. Tap **Join**.

## During the call

**MOST USED**

- **Mute** / **Unmute**: the room's microphones.
- **Turn camera off** / **Turn camera on**.
- **Sound**: room volume with **Quieter**, **Louder** and a slider.
- **Camera**: move and zoom the camera, or tap a preset such as **Wide** or **Table**.

**ALSO THERE**

- **Raise hand**, **Reactions**, **Captions on**.
- **Participants** and **Chat** (in Google Meet you can also send a message).
- **Mic**: turn it up if people in the call can't hear the room.
- **TV**: a remote for the TV, if you need it.

- **Pop-up questions.** If Zoom or Meet asks something (for example, to agree to a recording), a card appears on the table with its buttons. Tap your answer.
- **The next meeting.** When the next booking is 10 minutes away, the top of the table shows "Next: 10:00 AM …". The room doesn't hang up for you.

## Leave the call

1. Tap **Leave** at the bottom of the meeting card.
2. Tap **Tap again to leave** within 5 seconds.

*The table goes back to its main screen and the TV to its idle screen. **Always leave from the table**, even if the host has ended a Google Meet call, so the room is ready for the next person.*
