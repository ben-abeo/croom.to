# Conference room troubleshooting

*Part of [Conference rooms: getting started].*

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Join now button for my meeting | If it's more than 10 minutes early, wait: the button says when it opens. Otherwise the event has no Meet or Zoom link the room can use, or it's a Teams or Webex meeting. Paste the link into **Join with a link**, or see [Teams, Webex and laptop meetings]. |
| My booking isn't on the screens | Wait a minute or two. Check that the room was added under **Rooms**, that it didn't decline, and that the meeting is within 7 days. |
| "Couldn't join… Zoom needs the meeting passcode" | Type the passcode in the box that appears and tap **Join again**. |
| "Waiting for the host to let the room in." | Ask the host to admit the room (it appears as "Room 1" or similar). After 5 minutes the room gives up. |
| Zoom says the meeting isn't authorized | It's another company's Zoom meeting the room can't join yet. Join from your laptop and tell the administrator. |
| "Unsupported meeting link" | The room can't join this kind of call. See [Teams, Webex and laptop meetings]. |
| "A meeting is already in progress" | Someone didn't leave the last call. Tap **Leave** twice on the table, then join. |
| "That time was just booked in Google Calendar" or "That time is already booked" | Someone else got there first. Pick another time. |
| "Double-booked with…" | Two bookings overlap, usually after an internet outage. Talk to the other person and move or cancel one. |
| "Calendar offline since…" or "Not in Google Calendar yet" | Google can't be reached right now. Your booking is safe in the room and goes to Google when it can. |
| Nobody can hear the room, or the room can't hear the call | Check **Unmute**, then **Sound › Louder**, then **Mic**. If it says "No speaker found" or "No microphone found", tell the administrator. |
| The TV stays dark | Tap **TV** on the table and use **Power**, then **Input**. |
| "Can't reach the room" (table) or "Sign not connected" (door) | The room's system is restarting or offline. It reconnects by itself. If it lasts more than a few minutes, tell the administrator. |
| "The room is updating" | Wait a few minutes and try again. |
| "The camera is on the laptop: press Back to room first" | Tap **Back to room**, then join. |
| Settings asks for a PIN | Settings is for the administrator. You never need it to book or run a meeting. |

> **STILL STUCK?**
>
> Contact your Crystal Meet administrator. Say which room, what the table screen or door sign says (a photo helps), and what you were trying to do.
