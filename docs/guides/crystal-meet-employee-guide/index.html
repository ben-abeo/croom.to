How-to guide

Book the conference room and run your meeting from the table

Reserve the room, add a Google Meet or Zoom call, and join with one tap. Includes what to do for Teams and other calls.

Our conference rooms run on Crystal Meet. A touch screen on the table runs the call, the TV shows it, and a sign by the door shows who has the room. You don't need any technical experience, a login or a password. If you can create a Google Calendar event, you can do everything in this guide. Booking takes about a minute, and joining a call takes one tap.

Meet the room

The table screen

The touch screen on the table. You join calls, mute, change the volume and book the room here.

The TV

Shows the call. It turns on and switches to the right input by itself. You never need to touch it.

Camera and speaker

The Logitech MeetUp under the TV. It's the room's camera, microphones and speaker in one.

The door sign

The panel outside the door. It shows whether the room is free and lets you book it on the spot.

What the colours mean. The door sign's light bar and screen use the same four colours:

Green: free. Walk in or book it.

Yellow: starting soon. A booking begins within 10 minutes.

Red: in use. A meeting or booking is on now.

Grey or off. The sign can't reach the room. Tell the administrator.

Good to know before you start

The room never joins a call by itself. Someone in the room always taps Join now.

The room joins Google Meet and Zoom. For Microsoft Teams, Webex or anything else, see section 6.

Meeting titles are public. The title shows on the door sign and the TV. For a confidential meeting, use a plain title such as "Team meeting".

1

Reserve the room in Google Calendar

This is the best way to book ahead. The booking shows on the door sign and the table screen within about a minute.

  • Create your event in Google Calendar as usual: title, date, time and guests.
  • Add the room. Open the Rooms tab next to Guests (on the full event page, select More options first). Pick the conference room by name, for example Room 1.
  • Check that the room is available. Google shows whether the room is free at that time. If it's taken, pick another time or room.
  • Select Save. Google invites the room like any other guest.

Within a minute or two you should see your meeting in the Today list on the table screen and on the door sign. Meetings more than 7 days away appear once they're within a week.

Good to know

If the room declines your invite, someone else already has that time. The room ignores declined bookings, so pick another slot.

To change or cancel, edit the event in Google Calendar. Bookings made in Google Calendar can only be changed there, not on the room's screens.

Can't find the room under Rooms? A newly added room can take up to a day to show up in Google.

2

Book a room with Google Meet

  • Reserve the room as in section 1.
  • Leave the Google Meet video call on. Google Calendar adds Join with Google Meet to new events automatically. If it's missing, select Add Google Meet video conferencing.
  • Save. That's all. The room knows it's a Google Meet call.

The table screen shows "Google Meet" under your meeting's title. To join, see section 5.

Tip

Meeting in person, no call? Remove the Google Meet link from the event. Otherwise the room shows a Join now button for a call nobody needs.

Outside guests in a Google Meet call? If someone outside Crystal PM hosts the call, the room asks to join under its own name (for example "Room 1"). The host needs to let it in within 5 minutes.

3

Book a room with Zoom

  • Reserve the room as in section 1.
  • Add the Zoom link in one of these ways:
    • Use the Zoom add-on in Google Calendar to make it a Zoom meeting.
    • Or paste the Zoom invitation link into the event's Location or Description. It should look like https://us02web.zoom.us/j/85512345678.
  • Don't worry about the Google Meet link. When an event has both, the room uses the Zoom link you pasted. You can leave the Meet link or remove it.
  • Include the passcode. If the link doesn't already contain it, paste the whole Zoom invitation (with its Passcode: 123456 line) into the Description. The room reads it from there.
  • Save.

The table screen shows "Zoom" under your meeting's title. To join, see section 5.

Zoom links the room can't read

Personal links with a name, like zoom.us/my/jane.smith, don't work. Use the meeting link with numbers (zoom.us/j/…). Your Personal Meeting ID link in that form is fine.

Zoom meetings hosted by other companies may not join yet, because Zoom is still approving our room app. If the room says the meeting isn't authorized, join from your laptop (section 6) and let the administrator know.

4

Reserve the room at the door or at the table

Need the room right now, or want to book it while you're walking past? The door sign and the table screen both have Use now and Book. No login or PIN is needed. Bookings made here go into the room's Google calendar, but not into your own calendar.

Use the room now

Shown when the room is free.

  1. Tap Use now on the door sign or the table screen.
  2. Type Your name or the meeting's name.
  3. Pick how long. Each choice shows when it ends, for example 30 min · until 12:30 PM. You can't pick a time that runs into the next booking.
  4. Tap Start.

You should see "The room is yours until 12:30 PM." The sign turns red.

Book a later time

  1. Tap Book on the door sign, or Book room on the table screen.
  2. Pick the day: Today, Tomorrow or one of the next five days.
  3. Pick a start time. Only open times are shown, every 15 minutes from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
  4. Type your name or the meeting's name, and choose How long (15 minutes to 4 hours).
  5. Tap Book. To go back a step, tap Change time or Back.

You should see "Booked: your name, 9:00 AM to 9:30 AM." It appears in the Today list (or in This week on the door sign).

Finished early?

End now (table screen only)

For a booking made on the door sign or table: tap End now, then tap again within 5 seconds. This frees the room for others.

Plans changed?

Cancel

A booking made on the room's screens that hasn't started has Cancel beside it. Tap it, then tap Tap again to cancel within 4 seconds.

Good to know

Want a call with your booking? Bookings made at the door or table have no Meet or Zoom link. To run a call, paste the link into Join with a link on the table (section 5), or book in Google Calendar instead.

Everyone can cancel. Anyone at the room can cancel a booking made on its screens. If yours matters, book it in Google Calendar.

Left alone, the booking steps close by themselves after a minute.

5

Join and run your call from the table

Join a booked meeting

  1. Look at the table screen. Until 10 minutes before the start, the button reads Join opens at 9:50 AM.
  2. From 10 minutes before the start, tap Join now. You can also tap Join beside the meeting in the Today list.
  3. The table shows "Joining…". The TV turns on and shows the call in about 15 seconds.

The table switches to the meeting screen and shows the call's timer. If it says "Waiting for the host to let the room in.", ask the host to admit the room. They have 5 minutes.

Join a call that isn't booked

  1. On the table's main screen, tap the box Paste a meeting link or a Zoom meeting ID.
  2. Type or paste a Google Meet or Zoom link, or a Zoom meeting ID like 559 000 0559. Tap 123 on the keyboard for numbers.
  3. If a Passcode, if the meeting has one box appears, type the passcode.
  4. Tap Join.

During the call

Most used

  • Mute / Unmute: the room's microphones.
  • Turn camera off / Turn camera on.
  • Sound: room volume with Quieter, Louder and a slider.
  • Camera: move and zoom the camera, or tap a preset such as Wide or Table.

Also there

  • Raise hand, Reactions, Captions on.
  • Participants and Chat (in Google Meet you can also send a message).
  • Mic: turn it up if people in the call can't hear the room.
  • TV: a remote for the TV, if you need it.
  • Pop-up questions. If Zoom or Meet asks something (for example, to agree to a recording), a card appears on the table with its buttons. Tap your answer.
  • The next meeting. When the next booking is 10 minutes away, the top of the table shows "Next: 10:00 AM …". The room doesn't hang up for you.

Leave the call

  1. Tap Leave at the bottom of the meeting card.
  2. Tap Tap again to leave within 5 seconds.

The table goes back to its main screen and the TV to its idle screen. Always leave from the table, even if the host has ended a Google Meet call, so the room is ready for the next person.

6

Teams, Webex and other calls: use your laptop

The room joins only Google Meet and Zoom. A Teams or Webex meeting still shows on the door sign and the table, so everyone knows the room is taken, but it has no Join button. Pasting a Teams link shows "Unsupported meeting link". Reserve the room in Google Calendar as usual (section 1), then use one of these options.

If the table screen shows a Laptop button

Some rooms can hand the room's camera, microphones and speaker to your laptop.

  1. Plug the room's laptop USB cable into your laptop. Connect the TV's HDMI cable too if you want to show your screen.
  2. On the table's main screen, tap Laptop. If your booking is on, it switches straight away. If the room is free, choose a length under Use a laptop for and tap Switch to laptop.
  3. The table says "The camera, microphones and speaker are on the laptop until 10:30 AM".
  4. In Teams (or your other app), open the audio and video settings and choose Logitech MeetUp as the camera, microphone and speaker.
  5. When you're done, tap Back to room on the table. This also happens by itself when your booking ends.

If the TV doesn't show your laptop, tap TV on the table and use Input to pick the laptop's input.

If there's no Laptop button

  • Join from your laptop with its own camera, microphone and speakers, or a headset.
  • To show your screen on the TV, plug in the TV's HDMI cable if the room has one, and switch the TV's input with TV › Input on the table.
  • Please don't unplug the room's equipment. If you need the room camera on your laptop, ask the administrator.

Tip: sharing your screen in a Meet or Zoom call

The table screen can't share a screen. Run the call in the room as usual, then join the same meeting from your laptop and share from there. Mute your laptop's microphone and turn its speakers off first, or everyone will hear an echo.

Still stuck?

Contact your Crystal Meet administrator. Say which room, what the table screen or door sign says (a photo helps), and what you were trying to do.
